Part 63, A General Provisions
EEE Hazardous Waste Combustors
Abstract:
Q: Will EPA authorize, under 40 CFR part 63, subpart EEE, the use of data from a destruction and
removal efficiency test conducted on one kiln in lieu of a test on a second kiln at the same facility?
A: Yes. The company has demonstrated that the two kilns meet the stack test waiver criteria.
Therefore, EPA approves the request under MACT subpart EEE.
