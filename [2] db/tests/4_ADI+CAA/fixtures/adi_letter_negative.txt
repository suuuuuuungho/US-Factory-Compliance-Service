Comments: Partially rescinds the determination issued as ADI Control Number M080027.
Part 63, VVVV Boat Manufacturing
Abstract:
Q: May Stamas opt out of 40 CFR part 63, subpart VVVV applicability and rescind its Title V permit?
A: No. The facility remains a major source of HAP based on its potential to emit. Therefore, the
request to opt out of subpart VVVV applicability should be denied.
